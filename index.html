A Guide to GSAT English Composition

寫作技巧速覽

第一段：描述

  • 開頭：簡介圖片 A (+B) 內容。
  • 中間：描述 A/B 特色並解釋。

第二段：申論

  • 開頭：提出 1-2 個主要論點。
  • 中間：分別解釋論點 (Reason A/B + Explain A/B)。
  • 結尾：用約 1 句話總結。

學測英文作文高分結構

此題型分為 Part 1 (客觀描述) 與 Part 2 (申論觀點)，對應作文的第一段與第二段。

第一段：客觀描述與分析

目標： 客觀描述圖片、圖表內容，或比較兩者異同。

  • 開頭：
    直接總述圖片內容。
    範例：The pictures clearly illustrate two different lifestyles...
  • 中間：
    描述圖A與圖B的關鍵特徵，並使用轉折詞（如 However）進行對比。
  • 結尾 (可選)：
    一句話總結核心差異，為第二段鋪墊。
    範例：Obviously, these two images represent a strong contrast between...

第二段：申論觀點與闡述

目標： 提出自己的看法、偏好、原因或建議。

  • 開頭：
    旗幟鮮明地表達主要論點。
    範例：In my opinion, I would prefer the lifestyle shown in Picture A for several reasons.
  • 中間：
    分點論述，提出具體理由與解釋。
    • 論點一: First... (陳述理由) For example... (解釋或舉例)
    • 論點二: Second... (陳述理由) Specifically... (解釋或舉例)
  • 結尾：
    用1-2句話重申論點，強力收尾。
    範例：In conclusion, + [重述的論點]

高分轉折詞用法：從常用到精通

功能 一般用法 (4–5級分) 高分替代用法 (5–6級分) 選擇轉折詞查看專屬例句
1️⃣ 對比轉折 However, In contrast Conversely, By comparison, On the contrary, Whereas
However, rural areas offer a peaceful environment away from the city.
City life is often fast-paced and stressful. In contrast, country life is relaxing.
Some students thrive in competitive environments. Conversely, others perform better under less pressure.
Online shopping is incredibly fast. By comparison, shopping in physical stores takes more time and effort.
The exam was not easy. On the contrary, it was the most difficult one we've had this year.
Extroverts gain energy from socializing, whereas introverts recharge by spending time alone.
2️⃣ 順序列點 First, Second, Finally First and foremost, To begin with, Subsequently, Ultimately
First, developing a reading habit helps us learn new things.
Second, regular reading can improve our writing skills over time.
Finally, it allows us to relax after a long day of studying.
First and foremost, developing a reading habit significantly broadens our horizons.
To begin with, engaging in outdoor activities builds up our physical strength.
He graduated from college with top grades and subsequently found a job in a leading tech company.
Hard work, perseverance, and a positive attitude will ultimately lead to success.
3️⃣ 遞進補充 Moreover, Also Furthermore, In addition, Apart from this, Not to mention
Using smartphones before bed ruins our sleep. Moreover, it hurts our eyes.
Volunteering helps people in need. Also, it makes us feel happy.
Social media causes anxiety among teens. Furthermore, over-reliance on it negatively impacts real-life relationships.
Air pollution harms wildlife. In addition, it poses a severe threat to human respiratory health.
Learning a language requires time. Apart from this, it demands constant practice and patience.
The trip was physically exhausting, not to mention the fact that it rained all day long.
4️⃣ 總結結論 In conclusion All things considered, Taking everything into account, To summarize
In conclusion, protecting the earth is everyone's responsibility.
All things considered, the long-term benefits of a sustainable lifestyle far outweigh the minor inconveniences.
Taking everything into account, studying abroad is an invaluable and rewarding experience.
To summarize, effective communication and teamwork are the keys to solving complex global problems.
5️⃣ 表達觀點 I think, I believe From my perspective, Personally speaking, I am convinced that...
I think that doing experiments is better than just reading books.
I believe that students should have more free time to explore their interests.
From my perspective, academic grades alone do not define a person's true potential or worth.
Personally speaking, I prefer traveling independently rather than joining a guided tour group.
I am convinced that hands-on experiences are far more valuable than theoretical textbook knowledge.
6️⃣ 因果推論 Because, So Consequently, Accordingly, As a consequence, Hence
Because teenagers face immense pressure, they often don't get enough sleep.
Teenagers face immense pressure, so many of them suffer from sleep problems.
Teenagers today face intense academic pressure. Consequently, an increasing number of students suffer from sleep deprivation.
The school rules regarding smartphones have changed, and accordingly, students must adjust their habits.
He constantly neglected his studies to play video games. As a consequence, he failed all his final exams.
The forest habitats are being rapidly destroyed; hence, many species are facing extinction.
7️⃣ 舉例說明 For example To illustrate, A prime example is..., Namely, For instance
There are many ways to save energy. For example, we can turn off the lights when leaving a room.
Small actions can create significant changes. To illustrate, implementing a campus recycling program drastically reduces daily waste.
Many animal species are critically endangered. A prime example is the polar bear, which is rapidly losing its icy habitat.
The school plans to introduce two innovative courses next semester, namely, coding and artificial intelligence.
Consuming excessive junk food causes various health issues. For instance, it often leads to early-onset obesity.
8️⃣ 讓步強調 But, Still Admittedly, Undoubtedly, As a matter of fact, Indeed
Learning a new language is hard, but it is very useful.
The weather was terrible that morning. Still, the volunteers decided to go clean the beach.
Admittedly, mastering a second language requires immense effort, but the cultural rewards are unparalleled.
Undoubtedly, artificial intelligence has completely revolutionized the way we work and communicate.
He is not naturally gifted. As a matter of fact, he is simply one of the most disciplined students in the class.
Indeed, solving complex environmental issues requires immediate and unified global cooperation.
9️⃣ 條件假設 If, Although Provided that, Given that, As long as, In the event of
If we take action now, we can stop global warming.
Although the test was difficult, almost everyone passed.
Provided that we take immediate collective action, we can still mitigate the severe effects of climate change.
Given that our preparation time is extremely limited, we must prioritize the most urgent tasks.
You will eventually achieve your goals as long as you maintain a positive and resilient mindset.
In the event of an unforeseen emergency, please follow the evacuation signs calmly.
🔟 目的意圖 So that, To, For With the aim of, In an effort to, For the sake of
He studies hard everyday so that he can get into a good college.
Many teenagers join school clubs to make new friends.
We must protect the forest for the animals living there.
The government introduced strict new policies with the aim of significantly reducing carbon emissions.
Many proactive teenagers participate in debate clubs in an effort to step out of their comfort zones.
We are obligated to protect our natural environment for the sake of our future generations.

主題：Hunger Marketing vs. Discount Promotion

第一段（說明兩種手法）

In modern society, many businesses use various strategies to attract consumers and boost sales.

在現代社會，許多企業運用各種策略來吸引消費者並提升銷售量。

Two of the most common methods are hunger marketing and discount promotion.

其中最常見的兩種方式是「飢餓行銷」與「打折促銷」。

Hunger marketing creates a sense of scarcity by limiting the number of products or setting a short selling period, which makes customers feel the need to buy quickly before the product runs out.

飢餓行銷透過限制商品數量或銷售時間，製造「稀有感」，讓消費者覺得必須趕快購買，以免錯過機會。

In contrast, discount promotion lowers prices directly to encourage people to purchase more.

相較之下，打折促銷則是直接降低價格，鼓勵人們多買一些商品。

Both of these strategies aim to increase sales, but they work in different psychological ways.

這兩種策略的目的都是提升銷售量，但它們運用的心理機制不同。

However, which one is more effective depends on consumers’ habits and experiences.

然而，哪一種更有效，往往取決於消費者的習慣與經驗。

第二段（分析與個人觀點）

In my opinion, discount promotion is more effective in encouraging people to spend money.

在我看來，「打折促銷」更能有效地促使人們花錢購買。

First, people can clearly see how much they save, and that feeling of “getting a good deal” often motivates them to buy things they didn’t plan to.

首先，消費者能清楚看到自己省了多少錢，而「撿到便宜」的感覺常常會讓人買下原本沒有打算購買的東西。

For example, I once bought a pair of shoes just because it was 50% off, even though I didn’t really need it.

例如，我曾經因為鞋子打五折而買下一雙，其實我並不真的需要它。

Second, discounts can attract a wider range of customers, including those who are price-sensitive or not brand-loyal.

其次，折扣活動能吸引更多不同類型的顧客，包括對價格敏感或對品牌不特別忠誠的人。

While hunger marketing mainly appeals to fans of popular brands, discount promotion can easily increase sales volume in a short time.

飢餓行銷主要吸引的是品牌愛好者，而打折促銷則能在短時間內迅速提升銷售量。

To sum up, discount promotion is a more practical and direct way to make consumers spend more without much hesitation.

總而言之，打折促銷是一種更實際、也更直接的方法，能讓消費者毫不猶豫地掏出錢購買商品。

範文：113年學測

🧩 題目主題：青少年問題與機器人幫手

第一段：選定其中一個圖中的事情是你最想解決的。

第二段：說明你想要的「機器人幫手」要具備哪些特質或功能。

第一段：選定問題 + 原因說明

Among the three problems, I would like to solve mood swings.

在這三個問題中，我想解決的是情緒起伏。

Many teenagers, including me, sometimes feel angry or sad for no clear reason.

許多青少年，包括我自己，有時會莫名地感到生氣或難過。

This emotional change makes it hard to focus on schoolwork or get along with others.

這種情緒變化讓人難以專心讀書或與他人相處。

I believe it happens because we face too much pressure from studies and relationships.

我認為這是為我們面臨過多來自課業和人際關係的壓力。

第二段：機器人特質 + 協助方式

If I had a robot helper, I would like it to help me control my emotions.

如果我有一個機器人幫手，我希望它能幫助我控制情緒。

First, the robot could detect my mood through my voice and facial expression.

首先，這個機器人可以透過我的聲音和表情偵測我的情緒。

When it notices I am upset, it could comfort me with calm words or play my favorite music.

當它發現我心情不好時，可以用平靜的話語安撫我，或播放我喜歡的音樂。

Second, it could remind me to take deep breaths or go outside for a walk.

其次，它可以提醒我深呼吸或外出散步。

With its help, I could learn to manage stress and stay positive even when I face difficulties.

有了它的幫助，我能學會管理壓力，並在面對困難時保持正向的心態。

範文：111年學測

🧩 題目主題：不同的公園特色與理想公園

主題翻譯： 不同的公園有不同的特色與風格，請描述並說明你理想中的公園。

第一段 / Paragraph 1 — Description

Both pictures show different kinds of parks, but they have very different features.

兩張圖片都呈現了不同類型的公園，但它們有著截然不同的特色。

In Picture A, there is a colorful playground with slides and climbing frames. Many children might enjoy playing there.

在圖片 A 中，有一座色彩繽紛的遊樂場，裡面有溜滑梯和攀爬架，許多小朋友可能會喜歡在那裡玩耍。

In Picture B, the park looks more natural with many tall trees and a large green lawn. It seems quiet and peaceful.

在圖片 B 中，公園看起來更自然，滿是高大的樹木和一片寬闊的綠地，整體感覺寧靜而祥和。

While Picture A is lively and full of facilities, Picture B gives people a sense of calmness and nature.

圖片 A 熱鬧且設施豐富，而圖片 B 則帶給人一種平靜與親近自然的感覺。

第二段 / Paragraph 2 — Opinion and Explanation

In my opinion, my ideal park would be more like the one in Picture B.

在我看來，我理想中的公園會更像圖片 B 這樣的類型。

First, I prefer a natural environment because it helps me relax and forget stress. Walking under the trees and hearing birds sing make me feel peaceful.

首先，我喜歡自然的環境，因為它能幫助我放鬆並忘記壓力。走在樹蔭下、聽著鳥鳴，會讓我感到非常平靜。

Second, a park with open space allows families and friends to have picnics or exercise together, which makes people healthier and closer.

其次，一個有寬廣空間的公園能讓家人或朋友一起野餐、運動，這不僅能促進健康，也能讓彼此關係更親近。

To sum up, a quiet and green park is my favorite because it brings both physical and mental comfort.

總而言之，我最喜歡的公園是寧靜且充滿綠意的，因為它能帶來身心的放鬆與舒適。

🌿 高分改寫版作文：Different Types of Parks and My Ideal One

題目翻譯： 不同類型的公園與我理想中的公園

🟦 Paragraph 1 – Description（描述段）

As the pictures illustrate, both parks have distinct characteristics.

如圖片所示，這兩座公園各自擁有不同的特色。

功能說明： 引言句，開門見山點出主題「兩張圖都有不同特色」。

As shown in Picture A, the park is equipped with colorful playground facilities such as slides and climbing frames, creating a lively and cheerful atmosphere.

從圖片 A 可以看出，公園裡設有色彩繽紛的遊樂設施，如溜滑梯與攀爬架，營造出熱鬧又愉快的氣氛。

功能說明： 客觀描述 A 圖片，使用「as shown in...」比「In Picture A」更正式自然。

In contrast, Picture B features a more natural setting, filled with tall trees and a wide green field, giving visitors a sense of calm and freshness.

相較之下，圖片 B 呈現出更自然的環境，滿是高大的樹木與廣闊的綠地，讓人感受到平靜與清新。

功能說明： 使用高分轉折詞 In contrast，明確對比 A、B 兩圖的差異。

While the first park emphasizes fun and activity, the second one focuses on nature and serenity.

第一座公園著重於娛樂與活動，而第二座則強調自然與寧靜。

功能說明： 對比句型 While A..., B... 是學測高分作文中常見且成熟的句構。

🟩 Paragraph 2 – Opinion and Explanation（意見與說明段）

In my view, my ideal park would be closer to the one in Picture B.

在我看來，我理想中的公園更接近圖片 B 的樣子。

功能說明： 表達個人觀點開頭句，使用 In my view 比 In my opinion 更自然。

To begin with, I favor a natural environment as it allows me to unwind and escape from daily stress.

首先，我偏好自然的環境，因為它能讓我放鬆並擺脫日常壓力。

功能說明： 使用 To begin with 作為高分開頭，語氣流暢正式。

Moreover, an open and spacious park enables families and friends to enjoy picnics or engage in outdoor activities together, which strengthens relationships and promotes health.

此外，寬敞的公園讓家人與朋友能一起野餐或參與戶外活動，這有助於增進感情並促進健康。

功能說明： 用 Moreover 替代 Second，語氣更自然；搭配動詞 enables、engage in 展現語言多樣性。

All in all, a tranquil and green park appeals to me most, as it provides both physical relaxation and mental peace.

總而言之，我最喜歡的公園是寧靜又綠意盎然的，因為它同時能帶來身體的放鬆與心靈的平靜。

功能說明： 結尾句使用 All in all 與複合名詞 physical relaxation and mental peace，語感成熟且結構完整。

範文：115年預測題目

題目提示

每當重大災害（如地震、水災）發生時，總會造成嚴重的破壞與損失，但同時也激發出許多人性光輝的互助行動。請針對這個議題寫一篇英文作文，文分兩段。

第一段：根據上方兩張對比圖片，描述災害發生時可能出現的兩種不同情景。

第二段：說明你對於在災難中「互助」與「志願服務」的看法，並分享你相關的經驗或觀察。

第一段：對比兩種情景

When a natural disaster such as an earthquake or flood strikes, it often brings great destruction and loss to people’s lives.

當地震或水災等天災發生時，往往會對人們的生活造成巨大的破壞與損失。

In the first picture, the streets are flooded, and residents are struggling in knee-deep dirty water, carrying what little they could save.

在第一張圖片中，街道被洪水淹沒，居民在及膝的汙水中艱難前行，手上拿著僅能搶救出來的少許財物。

Their worried faces show fear and exhaustion, reflecting the hopelessness many victims feel during such disasters.

他們憂慮的臉龐顯示出恐懼與疲憊，反映出許多受災者在災難中所感受到的無助。

In contrast, the second picture presents a warmer scene.

相較之下，第二張圖片呈現出一個溫暖的畫面。

Volunteers wearing vests are smiling while distributing hot meals and bottled water to those in need at a temporary shelter.

穿著背心的志工面帶微笑地在臨時避難所中，將熱食與瓶裝水分發給有需要的人。

The people receiving the supplies look grateful and comforted.

接過物資的災民們面露感激與安慰之情。

These two scenes clearly show the dark side of disaster and the bright side of human kindness.

這兩個畫面清楚地展現出災難的黑暗面與人性的光輝。

第二段：個人觀點與經驗

In my opinion, the spirit of mutual help and volunteer work is one of the most valuable things we can see during a disaster.

在我看來，互助與志願服務的精神是災難中最珍貴的事情之一。

First, it gives people hope and emotional support when they feel lost.

首先，它能在受災者感到迷茫時，給予他們希望與情感上的支持。

When I saw news about the 2024 earthquake in Hualien, I was touched by how volunteers traveled long distances just to deliver meals and blankets to victims.

當我看到有關 2024 年花蓮地震的新聞時，我深受感動——許多志工遠道而來，只為了把熱食與毛毯送到受災戶手中。

Second, volunteering not only helps others but also teaches us the meaning of empathy and unity.

其次，志願服務不僅幫助他人，也教會我們同理心與團結的意義。

By giving our time or effort, we make our society more caring and connected.

當我們願意付出時間與心力，就能讓社會變得更加關懷與緊密。

To sum up, even though disasters bring pain and fear, they also remind us that kindness and cooperation can overcome any hardship.

總而言之，雖然災難帶來痛苦與恐懼，但也提醒我們：善良與合作的力量能夠戰勝一切困難。
